Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Who can use the free shuttles?

Anyone with a valid South Asian University ID card: students, faculty and staff.

How do I book a mini-bus seat?

Choose a route on the Plan a Trip page, select a departure and confirm your seat.

What if my bus is delayed?

Check the live map for updates, or call the helpline for help finding another ride.

Is there support for riders with disabilities?

Yes. Choose step-free access when planning, and request boarding support from the Help page.
