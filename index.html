SMARTER TRANSPORT PLANNING

Better services begin with understanding demand.

A demand-aggregation engine analyses travel requests to help university administrators identify high-demand routes and peak periods.

  • Understand when and where passengers need transport.
  • Plan shuttle routes and service frequency.
  • Improve vehicle allocation and operating schedules.
  • Use trip records and feedback to guide decisions.
